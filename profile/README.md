stella builds open-source tools for legal work: a legal workspace, legal data,
text-search engines, identifier validation, anonymization utilities, and shared compliance tooling.
Together they form an AI-ready ecosystem for legal data.

We focus on practical infrastructure for law firms and legal
technology teams.

Start with [`stella`](https://github.com/stella/stella) for the workspace, or browse
the smaller libraries if you need focused building blocks for legal data pipelines.

See [`stll.app`](https://stll.app) for the hosted version of the app.

Contributions welcome: open an issue or a PR, start a discussion, or join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/8dZjmVFjTK).
